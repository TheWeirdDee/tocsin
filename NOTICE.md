## IMS Bearing Data Set (NASA Prognostics Data Repository)

- Generated by the NSF I/UCR Center for Intelligent Maintenance Systems (IMS), University of Cincinnati, with support from Rexnord Corp.
- Distributed by the NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence Data Set Repository: https://www.nasa.gov/intelligent-systems-division/discovery-and-systems-health/pcoe/pcoe-data-set-repository/
- Citation: J. Lee, H. Qiu, G. Yu, J. Lin and Rexnord Technical Services (2007). "Bearing Data Set", IMS, University of Cincinnati. NASA Prognostics Data Repository, NASA Ames Research Center, Moffett Field, CA.
- Terms: no dataset-specific licence is published. The repository states that users employ the data at their own risk, and asks that publications acknowledge the repository and the data donors.
- What this repository contains from it:
  - two individual readings, in `web/public/samples/`;
  - features, decisions and display spectra derived from the data, in the release bundles and `results/`.
- The raw archives are not redistributed. `scripts/fetch_data.py` downloads them from NASA.
